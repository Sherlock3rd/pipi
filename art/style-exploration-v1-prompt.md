# Style exploration v1 generation record

Tool: built-in image_gen. Purpose: one six-direction 2D style comparison board; concept only, not production sprites.

Inputs: source photos 02, 04, 05, 07, 08 from reference/README.md, in that order. All eight photos were reviewed and retained locally; the generation endpoint accepts at most five references per call. The initial eight-reference request was rejected before generation; the retry uses five. No third-party artwork supplied to generation.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished 2D character art-direction comparison board for a desktop pet game. Landscape 3:2 high resolution. Six clearly separated equal cells in a 3 columns by 2 rows grid. Warm off-white paper background, thin quiet dividers, generous clear spacing, no decorative UI. Only text: small unobtrusive letter A B C D E F at upper left of each respective cell, reading order. No other words, numbers, dialogue, badges or watermarks.
ALL FIVE INPUT PHOTOS ARE SUBJECT AND BEHAVIOR REFERENCES of the SAME real blue-gray shorthaired pet cat; they are NOT style targets or backgrounds to reproduce. Preserve its actual identity: stocky yet naturally cat-shaped body, thick neck and broad cheek pads, round broad face with a short defined muzzle, upright medium triangular ears with gently rounded tips (not enormous kitten ears), solid dense charcoal/slate blue-gray coat (not bright cyan), golden lemon-yellow irises with dark pupils and a subdued olive inner tint, charcoal triangular nose, small closed mouth, thick tapering rounded tail, round weighty paws. No white bib, no white socks, no tabby stripes, no collar, no garments, no human body. Do not make it a generic smiling kawaii kitten.
Every cell shows the SAME THREE PHOTO-DERIVED POSES in the same layout, with ONE large primary seated three-quarter cat on the left (photo 2 body, slight curious head tilt and gaze from photo 8), plus TWO smaller full-body poses at the right: a tightly curled ball sleeping with nose tucked and tail wrapping body (photo 5), and a vertically elongated cat standing on rear paws with one forepaw raised and another lightly supported against a simple short vertical edge, head turned toward viewer (photo 7). Show complete paws and tail, consistent anatomy within style. Make shapes readable at desktop pet size. No elaborate background, no photographed interiors. Sleeping cat must look like same cat and be unmistakably asleep. Standing stretch is feline, NOT bipedal anthropomorphic mascot.
Six distinctly different 2D treatments, not six near-identical shaded drawings:
A (top left): refined hand-drawn cel animation, thin dark blue-gray clean contour, flat local colors and exactly one crisp shadow tone, restrained adult-cat proportions, expressive yellow eyes, no hair-by-hair rendering.
B (top middle): soft storybook gouache painting, matte dry-brush texture, broad painterly color masses, broken soft edges, minimal outline, plush rounded forms, entirely painted 2D.
C (top right): colored-pencil observational sketchbook illustration, visible graphite/colored-pencil hatching, delicate varied hand drawn contour, natural adult cat anatomy close to photos, muted blue-gray paper-grain shading, sparse strokes instead of photographic fur.
D (bottom left): bold cute sticker cartoon, thick dark slate outline, compact large round head and short rounded limbs, graphic flat fills, tiny simple highlight, strongly simplified silhouette but keep the real cat's round cheeks, yellow eyes and charcoal nose. NO white sticker border and NO 3D gloss.
E (bottom middle): retro editorial cut-paper 2D graphic illustration, no outline, limited 4-5 flat blue-gray and yellow colors, strong elegant interlocking rounded geometric shapes, no gradients, no textures, natural cat gestures. Clearly more graphic than B or A.
F (bottom right): authentic hand-placed-looking pixel sprite art, coarse crisp square pixel clusters with nearest-neighbor edges, small restricted slate-gray palette, golden eyes, no smooth lines, no antialiasing, no fake mosaic filter over a painting. Each cat reads like approximately 64-96 pixel sprite enlarged consistently.
General goal: original distinct art directions informed by 2D animation model-sheet, illustrative shape design and pixel-sprite workflows researched on ArtStation, NOT a copy of any artist or existing character. Absolutely no 3D rendering, sculpted toy, clay, volumetric studio lighting, photorealism, text speech, or glowing interaction circles. Uniform neutral presentation so the user can fairly choose a base visual style while recognizing their own cat and its poses.
Input mapping to the original eight-photo catalog: input image 1 = source photo 02 (front seated identity), input 2 = source photo 04 (relaxed side/belly pose), input 3 = source photo 05 (curled sleep), input 4 = source photo 07 (upright stretch and forepaw support), input 5 = source photo 08 (head tilt and gaze). All photo numbers earlier refer to this source catalog; do not reproduce room backgrounds.

## Output and review

Saved to `concepts/style-exploration-v1.png` (1536 × 1024). Six 2D directions and three consistent photo-derived poses per cell are present. This is an AI-generated art-direction board, not an animation atlas.
